rusqsieve

A world-class portable self-initializing quadratic sieve, compiled to WebAssembly and run in parallel across Web Workers. Accepts up to 1024-bit numbers, but SIQS sieving is limited at 400 bits; larger primes, perfect powers, larger composite made solely of small factors, are accepted.
